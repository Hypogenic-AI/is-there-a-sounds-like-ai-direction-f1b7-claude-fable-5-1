## Research question
Are MGT and HWT linearly separable in LM latent space, why, and can linear probes exploit it for detection? (p1 Intro)
RQ1 in-domain (5.2), RQ2 OOD (5.3), RQ3 why probes transfer / shared direction (5.4), RQ4 sample efficiency (5.5),
RQ5 does projection track degree of AI editing (5.6). Purely a READING/detection paper.

## Steering / causal experiments: NONE
- No activation addition, ablation, or generation-time intervention anywhere (main text, appendix, or code; grep for
  `steer` / `register_forward_hook` across `src/*.py` returns no matches).
- Steering only appears as a related-work mention (p2: probing vectors "can be used for detection, interpretation, or
  representation steering (Zou et al., 2025)").
- Limitations (p9) explicitly: results "do not establish the existence of a universal latent MGT direction.
  Representation spaces contain many potential confounding factors ... Future work should further investigate the
  stability, causality, and universality of MGT directions." => our causal question is stated as open.
- The extractor LLM only READS third-party text; it never analyses its own generations.

## Methodology (Sec 4, p5; App D)
- Extractor: Llama-3-8B (d=4096) main. [CODE] `llama_8b` = `meta-llama/Meta-Llama-3-8B-Instruct`, raw text, no chat
  template, tokenizer default special tokens, truncation max_length=1024, batch size 1, default dtype.
  Ablation models: Llama-3.2-1B/3B-Instruct, Qwen3-0.6B/4B/8B (+ "Qwen-32B" in Tables 7/9; not in the code's MODEL_DIR).
- Activation: residual-stream hidden state ("residual stream activations", p3) of the LAST TOKEN at every layer.
  [CODE] uses `output_hidden_states`, so includes embedding output: 33 states for 8B (figures show layers 0-32).
- Dim reduction: per-layer PCA fit on train activations, top 100 PCs (100/4096 = 2.4%).
  [CODE] a per-layer `StandardScaler` is applied BEFORE PCA (not mentioned in paper).
- Probe: sklearn LogisticRegression, L2, C=1 (max_iter=2000, seed 42). NOT diff-of-means.
  Diff-of-means only appears (a) as the RepreGuard baseline (1st PC of activation differences, mean-pooled, full
  space; footnote 21 p19 "often identical to difference-in-means") and (b) centroid lines in Fig 11.
- Probing vector: v = w/||w||_2 (unit norm). Score = h^T v (raw projection, no sigmoid).
- LLP (Layer-Averaged Linear Probe): one probe per layer; final score = mean of per-layer projections over all layers.
- CLP (Concatenated-Layer Linear Probe): one probe on concat of per-layer 100-d PCA features (L x 100).
- NOTE: the direction lives in standardized+PCA coordinates. To get a residual-space vector one must back-project
  (approx. `pca.components_.T @ w / scaler.scale_`) [my inference, not in paper/code].
- Sec 3.1 linearity test: MLP probes depth 1-5 on mid-layer reps; AUC falls as depth grows, ID and OOD (Fig 2, p3).

## Datasets and sizes (5.1 p5; B.1 p16-17)
- Representation analysis (Sec 3): M4GT (Wikipedia main; Reddit, PeerRead, ArXiv in App A.2).
  M4GT dropped for detection because results saturated.
- Detection: 4 benchmarks x 4 subsets = 16 settings; each 1,500 train / 500 test, label-balanced, random sample.
  [CODE] also a val split (size not stated).
  - DetectRL (domains): ArXiv, Reddit (writing prompts), Yelp, News (XSum); Task-2 domain data, mixed generators + attacks.
  - MultiSocial (languages): en, de, ru, zh; social-media text, mixed platforms/lengths.
  - RAID (generators): Cohere, GPT4, Llama, Mistral; mixed domains/decoding/attacks.
  - TSM (tasks, Wikipedia, English, 6 generators): First Paragraph, Paragraph Extension, Summarization, Text Style Transfer.
- AI-edit: APT-Eval (15k AI-polished texts; 200 sampled per polish level x 4 levels) and EditLens (same procedure;
  3-way experiment uses 1,000/200/200).
- Data is NOT shipped in the repo (expects `$BASE_COE/data/sets/<name>/{train,val,test}.jsonl` with `text`, `label`).

## Baselines (16; B.2 p17-18), mostly re-implemented on Llama-3-8B
Zero-shot: Likelihood, LLR, Rank, GECScore, Revise, RAIDAR, FastDetectGPT, Binoculars.
Supervised: EditLens (released RoBERTa-Large), ID (PHD, XLM-R), OpenAI-RoBERTa, RADAR, RepreGuard, BiScope,
TextFluoroscopy, fine-tuned RoBERTa-base (XLM-R-base for MultiSocial; bs 32, lr 2e-5, 2 epochs).

## Metrics and headline numbers
- Metric: AUROC throughout ([CODE] also AUPR, TPR@5%FPR, acc/F1 computed but not reported).
- In-domain (Table 1, p6): LLP DetectRL 1.000 x4; MultiSocial .957/.934/.921/.944; RAID .971/.993/.995/.993;
  TSM FP .971, PE .942, SUM .978, TST .896. Gain over best baseline +0.00 to +18.85 AUC points (largest on TSM-PE).
  Best baseline is usually fine-tuned RoBERTa; Binoculars strong on DetectRL/RAID, ~chance on TSM.
- OOD (Table 2, p7; mean of 3 within-benchmark transfers): LLP .98/.98/.99/.94 (DetectRL), .93/.88/.89/.87
  (MultiSocial), .93/.98/.99/.98 (RAID), .95/.81/.95/.86 (TSM). LLP beats best of 4 baselines everywhere, +0.39 to
  +11.89 (caption/abstract say "11.37"/"+11"; text says 11.83 - internally inconsistent). CLP loses on News (-1.40), zh (-1.69).
  OOD is only WITHIN a benchmark; no cross-benchmark transfer AUC is reported.
- Layers (C.6, Fig 15 p21): signal rises sharply by ~layer 5 (ID) / ~10 (OOD), then plateau; OOD degrades in final
  layers. Layer 16 = best OOD layer (used for vector-similarity analysis). PCA plots: separable from layer 6 (Fig 1).
- Sample efficiency (5.5, Fig 5): near-peak with 10-100 train samples; lower seed variance than RoBERTa/RepreGuard.
  PCA basis was still fit on the full train set (footnote 7); no-PCA rerun (Fig 12) gives same pattern.
- Ablations on TSM (Tables 7, 9 p22-23): mean pooling worse than last token (LLP -0.019 to -0.155; CLP up to -0.088);
  first layer only -0.32 to -0.43; last layer only -0.04 to -0.08; C in {0.01,0.1,10} ~no effect; k=10 hurts
  (-0.05 to -0.12), k>=100 flat, No PCA +0.007 to +0.016; Qwen-4B/8B/32B slightly better than Llama-8B; Llama-1B/3B
  only slightly worse. Tables 8/10: PCA vs no-PCA within ~0.01 on other benchmarks.
- Calibration (Table 6): ECE 0.000-0.010 on DetectRL.

## "Machineness spectrum" (5.6 p8-9; C.5, C.8 p20-22)
- Probe trained on binary labels; projection score correlated (Pearson) with edit-strength metrics between original
  human text and AI-polished version: semantic cosine sim (SciBERT CLS [CODE]), word-level Levenshtein, Jaccard.
- APT-Eval (Fig 6): LLP r = -0.586 (sem sim), +0.728 (Levenshtein), +0.710 (Jaccard);
  CLP r = -0.442, +0.584, +0.588.
- EditLens (Fig 14): LLP -0.494 / +0.609 / +0.613; CLP -0.496 / +0.609 / +0.604.
- [UNSURE] which dataset the probe was trained on for these correlations is not stated in the paper
  ([CODE] hints at targets `apt`, `apt_m4_train`, `editlens`, `beemo_*`; `apt_m4_train` suggests an M4-trained probe).
- 3-way (human / AI-edited / AI-generated) on EditLens, thresholds on val (Table 5): LLP acc .848, CLP .854,
  RoBERTa .652, EditLens model .525. Authors call it "preliminary".
- Correlational only: more-edited text projects further toward MGT. Nothing shows moving along the direction makes text more AI-like.

## Confound controls
- Domain / language / generator / task: handled only via OOD transfer (Table 2, Fig 10) and vector cosine (Fig 4).
  No explicit removal or orthogonalisation of a domain direction.
- Length: only a detection-vs-length ablation (C.7 Table 4, DetectRL ArXiv; text truncated to 25-400 chars): LLP
  .850 at 25 chars, .966 at 125, .997 at 400; RoBERTa slightly better <= 50 chars. No test of whether the direction
  encodes length; HWT/MGT length distributions not reported or matched.
- Formality / style: NOT controlled. Authors themselves attribute CLP's News failure to "overfitting to stylistic
  cues associated with formal writing" (p7) and list "adversarial attacks, text length, and stylistic or
  grammatical variation" as confounds explaining imperfect alignment, esp. Reddit (C.3 p20).
- Assistant persona: not discussed at all.
- Representation-geometry explanation (3.2, Fig 3/9): MGT has lower entropy, effective rank and intrinsic dim and
  higher anisotropy than HWT across layers; offered as "plausible explanation", not tested causally.
  GECScore baseline rationale (humans make more grammar errors) hints at a fluency confound; not examined.

## Limitations (p9, authors')
Only 4 geometry metrics; no systematic model-scale study; logistic regression only; universality/causality/
confounds not established; not benchmarked as a dedicated fine-grained edit detector; needs white-box access.
Mine: OOD only within benchmark; Fig 4 not quantified; last-token of raw text only.

## Code usage notes (`code/mgt_probes`)
- Entry points: `probe.sh` -> `uv run -m src.probes.probe_main --model llama_8b --dataset <name> --token_mode
  last_token --mode {pca|meta|default} --components 100 --ood {0|1} --folder <out>`.
  mode `pca` = LLP with PCA, `default` = LLP no PCA, `meta` = CLP. CLI default `--components` is 50 (paper: 100).
- Needs env `BASE_COE` (data in `$BASE_COE/data/sets/`, outputs JSON to `$BASE_COE/output/probe/<folder>/`).
- `src/inference.py`: `Inference.run` -> tuple of per-layer vectors; `token_mode` in {last_token, pooling (mean over
  tokens), all_tokens}. One text per forward pass, no padding.
- `src/probes/probe_main.py`: `_collect_model_states` (-> array n_layers x n_samples x d), `_train_linear_probe`
  (LLP), `_train_meta_probe` (CLP), `_evaluate` (normalise `coef_[0]`, dot with scaled/PCA'd test feats, mean over
  layers), `correlate_apt` (Pearson vs edit metrics).
- `src/probes/probe_ablations.py`: adds `--C`, `--seed`, `--mlp_depth`, training-size sweeps.
- Probes/vectors are NOT saved to disk (only metrics JSON); no pretrained directions released. Easy to reimplement:
  ~30 lines of sklearn. OOD group map `OOD` is imported from `src.utils` but is not defined there (import would fail
  as shipped) [CODE].
- Baselines in `src/baseline/` (Binoculars, FastDetectGPT, RepreGuard etc.) reusable as independent detectors.
